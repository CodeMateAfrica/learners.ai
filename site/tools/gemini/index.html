Gemini

AI assistant connected to Google Workspace, search, and multimodal workflows.

What it is

Gemini is Google's conversational AI assistant for writing, analysis, search, and multimodal work across Google products. It is most useful when a team already lives in Google Workspace and wants AI support that can work with documents, images, and browser-style research in the same flow.

Why it matters

Google's distribution gives it a large installed base inside enterprise and consumer workflows. The product matters because it sits directly alongside Gmail, Docs, and the broader Google ecosystem, which makes it a practical choice for organizations already standardizing on Google.

Workplace use cases

  • Drafting and polishing content inside Google Workspace documents
  • Synthesizing notes from meetings, email threads, and research into a brief
  • Getting a faster first pass on research questions and image-based tasks

Who uses it

  • Marketers
  • Product Managers
  • Executives & Founders
  • Operations
  • Researchers

How to use it

Open Gemini from the web or within Google Workspace, then ask for a specific deliverable such as a meeting summary, an outline, or a rewrite. It works best when you provide the source document or a clear set of constraints, because the quality depends on the context you feed it.

Key features

  • Google Workspace integration
  • Multimodal input for text and images
  • Fast research and drafting flows
